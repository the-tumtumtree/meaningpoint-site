IVI, a streaming platform, decided to refresh its tone of voice after many years without an update.

Communication at the company is highly diverse and handled by different people across different teams — product copywriters, marketers, social media managers, content editors, and customer support specialists. Each of them brings valuable experience, practices, and rules, some documented and some unwritten. So, to gather ideas and make sure important perspectives were taken into account, we conducted eleven interviews.

The new tone of voice guide consists of three parts. The first sets the overall framework and describes the brand's key qualities. The second contains general communication principles for IVI as a whole. The third brings together guidelines and examples for specific teams.

Most importantly, we wanted the final document to stay approachable and practical — something people would actually use in their everyday work. We hope we succeeded.
