The Riga Ghetto and Holocaust in Latvia Museum organised an exhibition, *Closed Borders*, about the migration crisis in Europe in the 1930s. The main exhibition was provided by the Jewish Museum in Moscow, but the Latvian version included an additional section: an essay about how the crisis affected Latvia, bringing the story closer to the local audience.

We developed the texts for the exhibition stands based on documents, photographs and personal stories collected by the museum team. By the way, you can visit [the virtual exhibition *Closed Borders*](https://rgmvirtualtours.lv/tours/borders/) at any time.
